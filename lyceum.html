Lyceum Background

Welcome to Lyceum Project, my private consultation project that distinguished offline/online discussion that converges in-depth discussions on data, mathematics, statistics and automation. The consultation tailored for any data practitioners especially those who lack a traditional data background from any industries. I recognize that the true meaning behind the numbers remains elusive, leaving us questioning the depth of our insights. Therefore, I aim to unravel the complexities of statistics, providing sessions to solving their problem by delve deeper and bridge the gap between formulaic calculations and meaningful interpretations.

Moreover, I acknowledges the repetitive nature of data-related tasks that can consume valuable time. In Lyceum, I emphasize automation as a solution, guiding client in streamlining their workflows and liberating them from mundane chores. By automating data processing, practitioners can channel their efforts into strategic thinking and creative exploration.

Lyceum serves as a safe space for individuals seeking to consult on their problem in project related to data. Not only that, I will help them enhance their statistical understanding, embrace automation, and ultimately unlock the full potential of their roles in the data landscape. Book my slot time now and together we dechipher information through data then solve your problem.

Session Details

I understand that not everyone is comfortable discussing data projects within a group setting, often due to concerns related to confidentiality or the need for a more conducive environment for focused work. Additionally, it's important to acknowledge that not all projects can be effectively addressed within the constraints of a 1 or 2-hour group discussion.

This is precisely why the Lyceum sessions will be conducted in a one-on-one format, offering personalized hands-on assistance, strategic insights, and the opportunity to explore the potential for more extensive, longer-term projects. You are encouraged to consider bringing the following common challenges to our sessions:

  • 🔹Analyze and extract actionable insights from structured or unstructured data
  • 🔹Implementing streamlined data pipeline automation
  • 🔹Formulating strategies to address issues within your SME business
  • 🔹Exploring solutions related to Text Mining and Image Processing
  • 🔹Charting a career path in the field of Data Science
  • 🔹Addressing specific technical concerns, including Python scripting and SQL
  • 🔹Learn theory about Descriptive or Inferential Statistics
  • 🔹Statistics, Machine Learning, and related topics
  • 🔹Tackling issues associated with tools such as Looker and Tableau
  • 🔹Discussing your Bachelor's or Master's Thesis topics
  • 🔹And other data-related concerns

Your privacy and specific needs are of utmost importance, and I look forward to providing you with a tailored and confidential consultation to address your unique data challenges.
